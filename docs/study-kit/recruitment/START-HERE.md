# Recruit and schedule six GEX sessions

These are blank organizer templates. No participant is enrolled, contacted or
scheduled by this package. The six CSV rows are planned assignments, not results.

1. Save a working copy of this organizer pack in private owner-controlled
   storage outside the repository and shared study kit.
2. Fill in the invitation's contact email, recruitment dates, incentive terms
   (or explicitly none, including early-stop terms) and consent link/attachment.
   Fill the consent form's contact, authorized reviewers, private-record deletion
   and removal-request dates before asking anyone to agree. Recording and
   AI-assisted analysis are separate optional choices, initially blank.
3. Send the invitation yourself. Use the confirmation and reminder templates
   after you agree on a date, time zone, setup and fixture. Copy completed consent
   into your private study records and give the participant a copy.

Send participants only the participant ZIP plus their personalized consent and
scheduling confirmation. The participant ZIP includes a blank consent template;
the separately supplied form with real organizer details supersedes it. Keep the
original ZIP unchanged and use the same delivery revision for all six sessions.

The checked application, dependency lock, task guide and synthetic captures are
byte-identical to the frozen study. Five links to omitted maintainer documents
in the shipped help are plain text in this delivery. Task content and scoring
are unchanged. The delivery amendment is recorded separately for study reporting.

Use session-plan.csv as a blank planning template. Three ES and three NQ
assignments are proposed; actual assignment, consent, dates and outcomes are
empty. Record actual changes, assistance, failed tasks, refusals and non-starts.
Keep contact details and identity mappings separately. Do not enter real people
or completed records into the repository's artifact folder.

Do not send the original full study ZIP: it contains moderator guidance, scoring
and rehearsal evidence. The participant-only ZIP excludes those materials.
For moderation, retain the existing frozen moderator guide and scorecard.

# Organizer checklist

This is an organizer-only recruitment aid for `guided-intake-2026-10-18-r1`.
The frozen study protocol, task IDs GI-01–GI-06 and scoring remain unchanged.
Do not include this file, moderator materials, scorecards, fixture answer keys,
or rehearsal results in the participant package.

## Fill in before sending anything

- Name the study contact and monitored contact email.
- Choose recruitment dates, proposed appointment windows, time zone and UTC offset.
- State the incentive amount and terms, or explicitly state that no incentive is offered. State what happens if someone stops early; do not imply compensation that has not been arranged.
- Choose a calendar date for deletion of all organizer-held private study records: contact and scheduling records, completed consent, ID links, individual notes, recordings and copies. Put that date and a removal-request deadline no later than it in the consent form. Completed consent and scheduling messages also link names to IDs; deleting only a mapping table does not remove those links. Aggregate findings without participant identifiers may be retained.
- Set the participant-only ZIP link and consent-form link or attachment. Check both from the recipient's access level.
- Decide who moderates and who can access identifiable observations. Audio recording, shared-window recording and ChatGPT/OpenAI analysis of de-identified written notes each require their own explicit Yes. A decline must leave a note-only, manually analyzed session available. Blank choices give no permission. Keep cameras and automatic transcription off; if silent window-only recording is unavailable, leave recording off. Do not upload participant names, contact details, audio, video or full transcripts for AI analysis.
- Keep recruitment replies, scheduling details, consent, identity mappings, observations and recordings in private owner-controlled storage outside the repository and shared kit. Do not paste actual participant details into these templates or commit them.

## Recruit and schedule

- Aim for six different consented participants who use ES/NQ or related trading research tools. Record the relationship of any project contributor; do not describe contributors as independent customers without that context.
- Ask only for role, relevant experience, OS/chip, Python availability, time zone and two or three availability windows. Access or setup needs are optional and do not require a diagnosis.
- Keep a private recruitment log that includes declined, withdrawn, ineligible, no-show and not-started contacts as well as completed sessions. Use de-identified IDs or counts in later reports; a decline does not require an explanation.
- Assign P01–P06 to the six planned sessions. Alternate ES `zero-gamma-flip` and NQ `nq-research-loop` so the planned assignments total three of each. Record an actual reassignment rather than silently replacing an inconvenient result.
- Confirm OS/chip and Python before scheduling. The rehearsed environment is Apple Silicon with Python 3.12.13; record a different environment separately or agree on a suitable setup. Do not claim that unrehearsed platforms are unsupported. Keep willing people whose setup differs in the private recruitment log.
- Check prerequisites ahead of time, but leave GEX installation for the observed task. If installation must be precompleted, record that circumstance; it cannot count as an unaided observed install.
- Confirm a 60–75 minute appointment with explicit time zone/UTC offset and a private meeting link. Retain reschedules, non-starts and refusals in the private recruitment record.
- Send only the participant ZIP, consent form, assignment and scheduling details. Do not send the full moderator study ZIP.

## Before each session

- Verify the participant ZIP and its checksum against the organizer handoff. Use the exact frozen build and assigned fixture. The source commit, wheel checksum, lock, protocol and fixture identities govern the cohort; version `0.6.0` alone is insufficient.
- Complete the organizer fields in the consent PDF (or edit its text alternative) before sending it, then obtain the observation choice before any tasks. Keep the original participant ZIP unchanged; send the completed-details consent separately and tell participants it supersedes the blank template. Make the removal deadline no later than the private-record deletion date. At deletion, remove or redact every private copy, including completed consent and scheduling records; retain only findings without participant identifiers. Confirm the separate recording choice before starting any recorder; keep automatic transcription off and start each kind of recording only after its corresponding explicit Yes.
- Ask the participant to close unrelated windows/notifications and share only the relevant application window. Do not collect account IDs, credentials, private trading records or personal/licensed captures.
- Open a blank moderator scorecard privately. Record the actual environment, assignment, build and all help. Keep the frozen success criteria and answer key with the moderator.

## Observe and follow up

- Run GI-01–GI-06 with the frozen participant instructions. Do not redefine a task, outcome or pass threshold mid-cohort.
- Record every intervention and retain failed, assisted, refused and not-started attempts. Leave unobserved values blank; do not turn preparation or automated rehearsal into participant outcomes.
- Let the participant pause, skip or stop. Honor recording withdrawal immediately. Follow the stated removal-request and private-record deletion dates, including contact/scheduling and consent copies.
- Keep identified contact/consent records separate from de-identified task observations. Do not put either into this public-facing artifact folder.
- After six actual sessions, analyze de-identified observations under the frozen protocol, including failures and assistance. A task, scoring, dependency, fixture or source change needs a new cohort identity; do not replace the September study or amend this frozen build to improve a result.

WHAT YOU WILL DO
In one remote session, you will install the supplied GEX build, share the
relevant application window, try six short research tasks and discuss your
experience. We use supplied synthetic ES/NQ data. The exercises need no live
feed, provider login or trading. Installing dependencies may require internet.

WHAT WE OBSERVE
We record task attempts, elapsed times, errors, help given, comments, relevant
research experience and computer/Python setup. We may keep synthetic test
receipts. Notes use a participant ID. Close unrelated windows and notifications;
do not show credentials, account IDs, positions, P&L, personal captures or
licensed market screens. You may pause sharing at any time.

STORAGE AND ACCESS
The organizer keeps contact details, consent and the ID link separately from
task notes in private owner-controlled storage, outside the public repository
and shared study kit. Only the contact and named reviewers above access
identifiable records. Deletion covers organizer-held contact and scheduling
records, consent, ID links, individual notes, recordings and copies. Aggregate
findings without participant identifiers may be retained.

PARTICIPATION AND REMOVAL
Taking part is voluntary. You may skip a question or task, pause or stop without
giving a reason. The incentive terms above apply, including an early stop. We
use the observations to improve the software; identifiable quotes or recordings
will not be published without separate permission. Contact the organizer by the
removal date to request removal of material linked to you. After the removal window closes, aggregate findings that no longer identify
you may not be separable for removal.

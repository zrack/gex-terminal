# Guided Session Intake — Moderator Guide

This supplement freezes `guided-intake-v1` tasks GI-01 through GI-06 in
`protocol.json`. [Product Validation](../product-validation.md) owns consent,
recruitment, metric definitions and scoring. Use this kit as a distinct cohort;
do not pool it into or alter the frozen September 20 study.

Before recruitment, choose the owner-controlled retention/deletion date and
obtain observation consent. Obtain separate recording consent if applicable.
Keep contact information, consent and all observations outside the repository
and handoff bundle. Assign de-identified IDs and retain refusals, failures,
assistance and not-started attempts. Do not count project contributors as
independent customers without recording that relationship.

Verify `SHA256SUMS`, the wheel identity and the runtime before use. Freeze the
exact wheel, lock, protocol and fixture identities with the cohort. A source,
dependency, fixture, task or scoring change requires a new cohort identity.
Automated installation/browser rehearsal is a preparation check. It does not
complete a participant row or establish clean-machine customer activation.

Alternate ES `zero-gamma-flip` and NQ `nq-research-loop`; record assignment with
every row. `fixture-answer-key.json` gives exact capture/source hashes,
instrument, multiplier, event count and selected final event cutoff. Both are
synthetic owned fixtures declared redistributable by their frozen catalog.
Do not substitute real captures or infer licensed-data rights from integrity.

For GI-03, ask the participant to select the final event checkpoint from the
verified capture. Match the cutoff to `last_as_of` in the answer key. Match the
explained structural level, position model and units to the receipt and frozen
model guide. Do not supply made-up numerical answers: ES and NQ have different
source coverage, and a missing OI or direction view is an honest unavailable
result. Legacy ES uses fallback time-to-expiry; NQ carries schema-v2 contract
fields. The selected input assumptions must remain inspectable.

For GI-04, OI, raw trade volume and directionalized volume must remain separate;
they are not additive. One parameter change should preserve the source cutoff
while changing the declared scenario. The participant should explain the input
change and any limitation without claiming a measured trading edge.

For GI-05, stop the first server. Launch a fresh server on port 0 with a new
empty workspace, reopen the receipt and confirm a successful verified
reproduction. Do not preload the original capture or checkpoint in that server.
This distinguishes a portable receipt from an artifact that silently depends on
the old process. Preserve source cutoff and scenario parameters in the result.

For GI-06, open the assigned bundled replay in Terminal and use `w`. Compare its
visible source, as-of, multiplier, selected model and assumptions with the
checkpoint in Wind Tunnel. The handoff freezes the selected Terminal state;
subsequent replay progress or settings do not update that checkpoint. Correct
limits: offline success proves neither live-provider reliability nor predictive
value. Tradovate issue #4 remains open for actual authorized provider payloads.

Log each intervention. After a blocking failure, assist only to expose the next
task and mark that task assisted. A preinstalled environment cannot count as an
unaided install. Use one blank scorecard per participant; empty timing or
outcome cells mean unobserved. Do not treat a withheld answer caused by a
misleading product label as a participant defect.

Retain actual source/OS/architecture/Python/build/fixture context and every help
count. Report individual times and raw denominators from the frozen Product
Validation definitions. Six actual observed sessions remain a future collection
milestone until consented participants perform these tasks.
